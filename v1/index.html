Every terrain. One fleet, working in order.

  1. 01 / 06 · Plot

    House build

    Footings dug, spoil hauled out, the pad levelled. On a tight plot, every machine is waiting on another one.

  2. 02 / 06 · Pipeline

    Pipeline trench

    A trench only moves as fast as the tippers come back. Grade sees the gap before the excavator stops.

  3. 03 / 06 · Highway

    Desert highway

    Long hauls on open sand. The grader follows the cut, so one late load holds up the finished road.

  4. 04 / 06 · Road clearance

    Mountain rock slide

    Rock across the only lane. The dozer clears, the excavator loads, the tippers take turns. Order matters more than speed.

  5. 05 / 06 · Access road

    Forest road

    One way in, one way out. On a narrow track through the trees, one tipper in the wrong place blocks everything.

What's built

    A physical sensor pod with a camera, 9-axis motion sensor, barometer and GPS, streaming live to a dashboard at 10 Hz.

  • Teleoperation

    Browser-based remote control of an excavator: sliders, keyboard jog and go-to-position, tested end to end on a simulated machine. A person drives it.
